About us

Tabaar Trading is a paddy and rice trading business based in Lahore, Pakistan. We work with a network of registered suppliers and settle every delivery on clear terms: the truck is weighed, a sample is tested in our lab, and the supplier receives the result with the deduction sheet it is based on.

What we do

From the farm gate to the mill, with every step recorded.

Paddy procurement

We buy paddy from farmers and suppliers across the region, weighed on our weighbridge.

Quality testing

Every truck is sampled and tested for moisture, broken, chalky, damage and more, against a published specification.

Rice trading & supply

We supply rice to buyers in agreed grades and quantities.

WhatsApp updates for suppliers
